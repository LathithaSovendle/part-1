Our Programs and Upcoming Events 👾

Helping Hands Foundation runs several community based programs aimed at supporting children, families, and individuals in need.
Each program is designed to make a positive impact and promote well being in our local community.

After School Support

Provides children with homework help, tutoring, and a safe space to learn

Food and Essentials Drive

Collects and distributes food, hygien items, and basic
supplies to families facing difficult times.

Youth Mentorship

Connects young people with caring mentors who help
express themselves and explore their talents.

Upcoming Events

Helping Hands Foundation hosts regular events to engage the community, raise awareness,
and support our causes.

Annual Fundrasing Walk

A community to raise funds for our food program. Held every August,
this event brings volunteers and donors together for a great cause.

Winter Clothing Drive

We collect and distribute warm clothing to those in need each winter.
Drop off points are set up cross the city during November and December.
